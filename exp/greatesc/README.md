# Camp Escape — a study of *The Great Escape* (1986) in MTE MK1 v3.2

A top-down ZX Spectrum 48K rendition of the design of Denton Designs / Ocean's
*The Great Escape*, made with **MK1 v3.2** (`mojontwins/mk1v32`) and z88dk 1.10.
The graphics are new top-down drawings in the spirit of the original. None of
them are taken from the tape. Most of the game logic is in the **MSC4** script
(`script/script.spt`, fully commented). The rest is about 150 lines of C: the camp clock
in `dev/custom.h`, and the text box plus `EXTERN` hooks in `dev/extern.h`.

`show/greatesc.tap` is the built game. Controls: `1` keyboard (`O P Q A`, `SPACE`
= action), `2` Kempston, `3` Sinclair.

## What is carried over from the original

| Original | Here |
|---|---|
| Daily routine: roll calls, breakfast, lights out, bell | Camp clock (TIME in the HUD). Miss an appointment and the alarm rings. |
| Out of place = guards arrest you | Alarm on, or restricted area (barracks, office, gate): touching a guard or dog is an arrest. |
| Morale, lost when caught | MORALE (the engine's life counter), -10 per arrest, +5 per achievement, 0 = game over. |
| Solitary confinement, items confiscated and returned to their places | The cooler (screen 29). Confiscated or dropped items reappear where they were found. |
| Carry two items | Two inventory slots in the HUD, drawn by the script. |
| Tunnel under the stove, torch, shovel | Shift the stove, light the tunnel with the torch, dig out a roof fall with the shovel. |
| Wire cutters on the fence | Cut a hole in the north fence (it stays cut). |
| Guard's uniform + papers through the gate | Uniform makes restricted areas safe. Uniform + papers gets you out through the gate. |
| Compass + papers needed once outside | Without both you are recaptured in the woods. |
| Red Cross parcel, key, lockpick, bribe, food for the dogs | All present. The parcel holds the compass. |

## Map (6 × 5 screens)

```
 0  1  2  3  4  5    outside the wire (any of them = escape attempt)
 6  7  8  9 10 11    shed, Hut 1, Hut 2, mess hut, Red Cross store, NE corner
12 13 14 15 16 17    storeroom yard, parade W/E, barracks*, office*, gate*
18 19 20 21 22 23    interiors: Hut 1 (start), Hut 2, mess, store, barracks*, office*
24 25 26 27 28 29    tunnel (24-28), the cooler (29)          * restricted
```

## Editing

* **Map, enemies, tile behaviours:** `apor/greatesc.apor` (Aportador). On Linux,
  `comp.sh` exports it by itself (`utils/py/apor2mk1.py`, same output as
  Aportador's *Exportar*) and copies the behaviours into `config.h`. On Windows,
  press *Exportar* in Aportador before `comp.bat`. Enemy types: 1 guard, 2 dog,
  3 officer, 4 guard with torch. Hotspots are not used: the script places all items.
* **Graphics:** `gfx/speccy/*.png`. The originals are generated by
  `gfx/src/make_gfx.py`; edit the PNGs directly or change the script and rerun it.
  The font's `< = > # $ ' ( )` glyphs are text-box frame pieces.
* **Logic:** `script/script.spt`. The header comment has the item table, the
  routine and the MSC4 pitfalls found while writing it.
* `map/src/make_apor.py` is the one-off generator of the first `.apor` (the
  map drawn in ASCII). You don't need it any more.

## Building

**Linux:** `cd dev && Z88DK=/path/to/z88dk10-orig ./comp.sh`
(`./comp.sh justcompile` recompiles only the script and C). The tools are
Python 3 + Pillow ports in `utils/py`:

| Python | Original |
|---|---|
| `zxgfx.py ts2bin / sprcnv / png2scr` | `ts2bin.exe`, `sprcnv.exe`, `png2scr.exe` (same colour rules and layout) |
| `mk1data.py rle53map / ene2h / mktap` | `rle53map_sp.exe`, `ene2h.exe`, `bas2tap.exe` + `bin2tap.exe` |
| `zx0.py` | `zx0.exe` (greedy parser, ~5% bigger; each run is verified by decoding it with a bit-exact model of `dzx0_standard`) |
| `msc4.py` | `msc4.exe` v4.6.20260729 (line-by-line port, interpreter templates verbatim) |
| `apor2mk1.py` | Aportador's export (+ an optional full-map preview PNG) |

`utils/build_z88dk_linux.sh` explains how the Linux z88dk was built. The
compilers come from z88dk's git history (Dec 2012). The libraries, includes and
custom `spec_crt0` come from `env/z88dk10-orig.zip`.

**Windows:** the usual: `z88dk10-orig` on `C:\`, then `cd dev` and `comp.bat`
(uses the `.exe` tools in `utils`).

## Testing

`utils/py/zxrun.py` drives a snapshot in skoolkit's C Z80 simulator with
scripted keys, teleports, flag pokes and screenshots. `tests/*.txt` are the
scenarios used during development: tunnel route, winning by the tunnel,
guards/dogs/uniform, wire cutters, bribe and gate, night clock. To get a snapshot:
`tap2sna.py --sim-load-config finish-tape=1 show/greatesc.tap ge.z80`, then
`zxrun.py ge.z80 tests/02_win_by_tunnel.txt dev/greatesc.map`.

## Notes on the engine and tools found along the way

* `msc4.exe` emits `read_addr` only for TEXT BOX / DIALOG, but `CALL` needs it
  too. The port emits it for `CALL` as well, and reports `CALL` to an undefined
  sub (the original silently produced a bad address). The `HIDE_HOTSPOTS`
  rvalue handler in the original has a label typo. The port fixes it.
* Section headers are tokenised with `_` as a separator, so a `SUB` name with
  `_` never matches its `CALL`. `THEN`/`END` lines can't carry comments.
  Literals are 0–254 (255 is the indirection marker).
* The committed `script.spt.bin` files in `games/` predate the current
  compiler. They still emit the clause terminator after `BREAK`, so they don't
  match a fresh build.
* The 5-bit text decoder outputs `, . ? !` and space as the characters 91–95.
  The font must have those glyphs in the `[ \ ] ^ _` slots.
* `__FASTCALL__` parameters can't be read by name in sccz80. Take them from HL
  in asm, as Papaya does.
* Sprites take the INK/PAPER of the cells under them. On cells whose light
  colour is the paper, figures become invisible. That's why the tunnel is red
  earth and not black.
* One small engine touch: `pantallas.h` prints "MK1 V3.2" on the title unless
  `NO_TITLE_VERSION` is defined.
