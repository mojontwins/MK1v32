# Camp Escape - walkthrough

Screen numbers as in the README map. Doors: walk into the dark doorway at the
bottom of a building. Action = SPACE. Keep an eye on TIME: be on the parade
ground (14) at 08, 14 and 20h, in the mess hall (20) at 09h, and in Hut 1 (18)
from 21h. You have about half an hour of grace to get there.

1. Tunnel first (07h). Leave Hut 1 and go west to the shed (6): take the
   SHOVEL from the crate nook. Go east to the mess hut (9) and take the TORCH
   (top left inside). Back in Hut 1, stand below the stove (top right) and
   press action: the trapdoor appears. Step on it: the torch stays in the
   tunnel, so it is lit for good. Walk east to the roof fall (26), stand next
   to it and press action: the shovel breaks, but the way is clear. Go back up
   the ladder at the start (24). Your hands are empty again.
2. Roll call at 08 on the parade ground (14), breakfast at 09 in the mess (20).
3. The LOCKPICK is in Hut 2 (8, by the lockers, top right).
4. The commandant's office (16) is restricted: get in and out quickly, keeping
   clear of the guard outside and the officer pacing inside. Take the KEY
   (floor below the desk). Stand below the safe (top right) and press action:
   the lockpick snaps, the safe opens. Take the PAPERS. You now carry key +
   papers.
5. Red Cross store (10): walk into the door and the key opens it. Take the
   parcel in the middle: it holds the COMPASS. You now carry papers + compass.
6. Make the 14h roll call, then go to Hut 1, down the trapdoor and east
   through the tunnel to the ladder at the far end (28). Climb up into the
   woods: free!

Other ways out
- Wire: WIRE CUTTERS are in the crate box in the storeroom yard (12, top
  left). Stand just below the north fence in any screen from 6 to 11 and press
  action. The hole stays cut. Come back with compass + papers and walk north.
- Gate: the UNIFORM is in the guards' barracks (15, inside at the top).
  With uniform + papers, stand next to the gate (17, east side) and press
  action: the sentry lets you out. (The compass isn't needed this way.)

Tips
- Carrying the uniform makes restricted areas safe, but not an alarm.
- The BRIBE (Red Cross store) cancels one arrest. FOOD (mess hall) puts a
  dog to sleep for good.
- An arrest costs 10 morale and everything you carry. Items always go back
  to where you found them, so nothing is lost for good.
- Sleeping in Hut 1 makes the night pass four times faster.
