# Nanako in Classic Japanese Monster Castle - beeper sound effects for the idle
# time player (dev/sfx_player.h). utils/sfxcnv makes dev/sfx.h from this file.
#
#   sfx name [priority]          SFX_NAME = its number, in order
#       tone  frames pitch [slide]
#       noise frames pitch [slide]
#       rest  frames
#
# frames: 50 Hz ticks. pitch: Hz ("440") or period ("p40", 1-245).
# slide: added to the period each tick (+ goes down, - goes up).
# A higher priority effect can't be cut by a lower one.
#
# 0-5 are MK1's (peta_el_beeper 0-5 in the engine); 6-9 are Nanako's, in the
# slots of MK1's shot, item, key and life (not used here): the CPC player has
# 10 effects. The same numbers are the AY effects on the ZX 128K and the CPC
# (tools/nanako_music.py): jump, take, drop and death are the original's.
